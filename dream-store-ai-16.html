Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Dream Store AI stores your account details (email, password) and the dreams you submit directly on your device, in your browser's local storage. This data is not currently sent to or stored on any Dream Store AI server.

When you weave a dream or generate a video, the text of your dream is sent to third-party AI providers (Anthropic and fal.ai/Kling) solely to generate your story, interpretation, and video. These providers process the request and return the result; Dream Store AI does not control how long they retain that data — refer to their respective privacy policies for details.

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. Clearing your browser data will remove your saved account and journal entries from this device.

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app owner via the contact details on the About page.
